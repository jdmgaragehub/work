## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

1. Distinguish experimental, correlational and observational designs and the conclusions each allows.
2. Identify independent, dependent and confounding variables in a proposed study.
3. Apply consent, withdrawal, debriefing and risk–benefit principles when judging a proposal.
4. Use a power analysis to justify a sample size.

## Procedure in the game

1. Enter the **Methods & Stats Library** and open the **Research Methods Lab** (`methods`). Classify the three example studies by design.
2. Open the **Experiment Builder** (`builder`). Create a two-condition variant of the Stroop task (`stroop`), e.g. a change in word list or colour set.
3. Fill in the builder's consent text, withdrawal statement and debrief. Save the design.
4. Open the **Ethics Board (IRB)** (`irb`). Judge at least three proposals (approve / revise / reject) and record the board's reasons.
5. Return to the **Statistics Workbench** (`stats`) and run a **power analysis** (`power`) for your own design.
