## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

1. Explain consent, confidentiality and its limits at the start of a session.
2. Identify and use OARS micro-skills (open questions, affirmations, reflections, summaries).
3. Recognise when referral is needed.
4. Describe how culture shapes the expression of distress and help-seeking.

## Procedure in the game

1. Press **4** for Clinic. Complete "A Client's First Session" journey: waiting room, intake, consent and confidentiality limits.
2. Hold a full session with a **simulated** client. All clients are fictional; ZEHN never screens or labels you.
3. After each reply, note the micro-skill you intended and the skill the supervisor scored.
4. Read the supervisor note and the referral information. Open **Therapies & Counselling** (`therapy`) and **Cross-Cultural & Indigenous** (`crosscultural`).
5. Run a second session with a different simulated client.
