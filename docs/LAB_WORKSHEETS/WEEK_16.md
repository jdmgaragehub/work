## Procedure in the game

1. Open the **Experiment Builder** (`builder`). Write and save a preregistration: hypothesis, design, sample size (from a power analysis), exclusion rules and planned test.
2. Open **Replication & Open Science** (`replication`) and choose a finding to replicate (e.g., **levels of processing**, `levels_processing`).
3. Run the replication with simulated participants (plus yourself if you wish). The outcome reflects the finding's replication status in the fact base.
4. Analyse in the **Statistics Workbench** (`stats`) exactly as preregistered.
5. Generate a report in the **APA Report Generator** (`report`). Prepare your poster in **Present & Report** (`present`).
6. Read **AI in Psychology & Ethics** (`aiethics`) and note how you used (or did not use) AI tools.
