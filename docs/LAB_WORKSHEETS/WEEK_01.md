## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

1. Describe how early laboratory psychology tried to measure mental processes with reaction time.
2. Explain the logic of the subtractive method: the extra time a choice takes estimates the time of the extra mental step.
3. Compare how structuralist, functionalist, behaviourist and cognitive psychologists would interpret the same RT data.
