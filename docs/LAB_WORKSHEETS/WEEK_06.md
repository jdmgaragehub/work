## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lab you will be able to:

1. Estimate a difference threshold with an adaptive staircase and compute a Weber fraction.
2. Separate sensitivity (*d*′) from response bias (*c*) using hit and false-alarm rates.
3. Explain why illusions show perception is an interpretation of cues.

## Procedure in the game

1. Press **3** for Participant Mode. In the Experimental Lab Wing, open **Sensation & Psychophysics** (`sensation`).
2. Run the **JND staircase** (`jnd_staircase`) at two standard intensities. Record the threshold (mean of the last reversals) for each.
3. Open **Signal Detection** (`sdt`). Run **yes/no SDT** (`sdt_yesno`) under a neutral payoff, then under a liberal payoff.
4. Record hits, misses, false alarms and correct rejections for each block.
5. Optional: measure the **Müller-Lyer** illusion (`muller_lyer`) in **Perception & Illusions** (`illusions`).
